My Favorite Movie

The School for Good and Evil · 2022 · “There are two sides to every story”

Clip file: media/movie-clip.mp4

Synopsis

Best friends Sophie and Agatha are swept away to a magical school that trains the heroes and villains of fairy tales. Sophie, who has always dreamed of being a princess, is sent to the School for Evil, while Agatha, the outcast, lands in the School for Good. To find out where they truly belong, and to save their friendship, they have to see past the labels. It is the perfect movie for the idea that there are two sides to every story: good and evil are not always what they seem.

Cast

  • Sophia Anne Caruso as Sophie
  • Sofia Wylie as Agatha
  • Charlize Theron as Lady Lesso
  • Kerry Washington as Professor Dovey
  • Michelle Yeoh as Professor Anemone
  • Laurence Fishburne as The School Master

Characters

SophieA dreamer who longs for a fairy-tale ending. She is sure she belongs with the princesses, and her journey shows how complicated “good” can be.
AgathaSophie’s loyal best friend and a self-described outcast. She is quietly brave, and she proves that real goodness is about heart, not looks.
TedrosThe charming prince of the School for Good. He is expected to be the perfect hero, and he has to decide who he really wants to be.
Lady LessoThe stern dean of the School for Evil. She is powerful and mysterious, with a past that ties into the heart of the story.
